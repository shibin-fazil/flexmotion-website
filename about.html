About FlexMotion

We build robots that find their own way through your facility.

FlexMotion exists because indoor delivery in hospitals and hotels has stayed manual while everything around it has automated. We're changing that with autonomous mobile robots — not fixed-path AGVs — designed for the reality of shared, crowded, indoor spaces.

Our Mission

Free up staff time. Keep service moving.

Front-desk teams, nurses, and kitchen staff lose hours every day to walking trays, supplies, and amenities from one point to another. FlexMotion's AMRs take over that repetitive movement — autonomously, reliably, and without disrupting how the facility already runs.

We're currently focused on two sectors where this problem is most acute: hospitality and healthcare — environments with tight corridors, elevators, constant foot traffic, and a real need for precision.

How It Works

Core capabilities

Everything FlexMotion ships is built around one idea: the robot should adapt to the building, not the other way around.

Autonomous navigation (SLAM)

Robots map and localize within the facility on their own — no tracks, tape, or fixed infrastructure required.

Precise point-to-point delivery

Kitchen to room, station to ward, reception to door — every route ends at an exact, repeatable destination.

Motion Galaxy fleet platform

A central admin system to assign tasks, monitor bots, and manage the whole fleet from one screen.

Auto & manual charging

Robots return to charge automatically between tasks, with manual override when needed.

Guest & visitor assistant screen

An onboard screen welcomes, guides, and assists guests or patients at pickup and drop-off.

Robots as a Service

Deployed with low upfront investment and a promised 5–6 month ROI — no large capital outlay.
